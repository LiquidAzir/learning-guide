# Research selection

The feeds are curated files, not automatic news imports. Select entries for the explanatory value of their question, evidence, and limitations. Preserve original titles, primary links, accurate publication dates, and claim labels. Never make an old source look newly published. Month-only dates retain month precision; `verified` records a source check, not replication.

Scheduled discovery collects unreviewed metadata separately from the site. Follow [the review workflow](research/REVIEW.md) before adding candidates to a feed. Newly reviewed entries require an explicit method, limitation, four-part explainer, and useful background link. `added` records first inclusion in the guide; leave it absent for legacy records whose addition date is unknown. A discovery run is not a collection update, and a successful metadata request is not a source review.

## Political Science and Institutions

Include research on institutional design, voting rules, administration, collective action, accountability, public services, and international cooperation. A useful entry teaches a transferable mechanism or a method for evaluating one.

Require the primary research paper, transparent dataset, or substantive technical report. A journal name is not sufficient evidence of quality. Read the methods and available results; identify the population, comparison, outcome, uncertainty, and limits. Distinguish empirical findings, formal mathematical guarantees, and research proposals. Do not present a normative judgment as something the data alone established.

Every political-science entry must have `evidence.question`, `evidence.method`, and `evidence.limitations`, displayed in Source details and validated during the build. Its readable summary must explain the finding and a material limitation. Source formats are limited to journal publications, conference papers, preprints/working papers, technical reports, and data/tracker resources. These structural checks support editorial review; they cannot establish neutrality or research quality automatically.

Exclude campaign coverage, daily opinion or election polling, personality disputes, scandal roundups, punditry, party messaging, fundraising material, and advocacy press releases. Do not import a news feed and try to clean it with keyword filtering. Institutional research involving contested questions can qualify on the same evidentiary standards regardless of which side likes its conclusion. Do not force artificial partisan balance or exclude sound null results, corrections, and replications.

No daily quota. If no worthwhile study qualifies, add nothing. Keep separate studies and their settings distinguishable; one result is not a verdict on an entire political system.

## Psychology, neuroscience, and consciousness

Separate behavior, neural measurement, proposed mechanism, and subjective experience. State when evidence comes from an animal model, one participant, a clinical sample, or a specific task. Brain decoding is not unrestricted thought reading. A correlate or theoretical prediction is not a universal consciousness test. Give competing theories their actual predictions and report important challenges without announcing a winner the evidence does not establish.

## Engineering

Attach the operating conditions and system boundary to performance claims. Distinguish simulation, material, component, prototype, and deployed system; electrode, cell, and packaged battery; efficiency and lifetime; independent certification and manufacturer reporting. Retain cost, durability, scale, and integration limitations when relevant. A research programme page must be labeled as such and must not be summarized as a completed result.
