Add in the Meta AI app

Open this page on your phone with the Meta AI app installed and your Meta Ray-Ban Display paired.

Or add the link yourself

  1. Enable Developer Mode for your Display glasses in the Meta AI app, if it is not already enabled.
  2. Open your glasses settings → App connections → Web apps → Add a web app. Labels may vary by app version.
  3. Name it Learning Guide and paste the link above. Open it from the apps on your glasses.

Reading controls

Use up/down to choose a button and pinch to select. While reading, left/right turns the page. Select Menu for chapters, text size, or the current section list. On a computer, use the arrow keys and Enter.

Reading positions are saved on that device when storage is available. They do not sync with your phone. An internet connection is needed to open new chapters. Equations, references and diagrams are retained; intricate figures are easier to inspect in the regular guide.
