## How to use this

Each entry gives dates, what the person did that matters for this guide, and the chapter where they appear. The actors come first in each section, then the historians whose work the section rests on, because a reader who wants to go further should know whose books to look for. Names are in the forms used in the chapters; East Asian names are given family name first, as in the chapters.
